## 1. PLEDGE: choose what a smaller 3D scene may lose

[PLEDGE v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17605v1), submitted October 13 at 02:25:08 UTC, turns user tolerances into per-splat compression bounds. The [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17605v1) tests 13 scenes and reports smaller files at 11 of 19 comparable training-free baseline points. The interesting contribution is explicit control of error, not a universal compression win.

Its guarantees are relative to the pruned model and a declared camera domain. Footprints follow affine rasterization; composited color bounds require fixed geometry and opacity. Public implementation was not verified, and no codec was run.

**Best new visual pairing:** quantize colored splats, move the camera and inspect screen-space displacement. Controls: precision, near plane, focal length, frustum half-angle and tolerance. Separate pruning, omitted primitives and quantization errors. Status: proposed analytic lab, not a PLEDGE reproduction or measured file-size benchmark.

## 2. PhysicsSliders: a useful sound-control counterexample

[PhysicsSliders v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18597v1), submitted October 13 at 15:53:45 UTC, trains a flow model on simulated contact sounds. The [study](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.18597v1) distinguishes a slider moving sound in the right direction from actually hitting two targets. A joint-control grid covers 225 setting–run evaluations. Supplying thickness improves joint frequency/damping success from 27.6% to 68.0% versus masking it. Success requires at least two of four outputs within 1.5× of both eight-output simulator-median targets.

A separate real-audio target evaluation uses 240 requests across five datasets and three training runs. Sustained-sound target ranges remain compressed; control cross-talk, measurement dependence and missing perceptual evaluation limit the claim. Code, weights and a runnable demo were not verified.

**Visual proposal:** coupled size, thickness and damping sliders beside synthetic resonances and decay curves. Compare requested and achieved targets, with a hidden-thickness toggle. Status: proposed; synthetic audio would not be paper output. This is a Foley and sound-design topic.
