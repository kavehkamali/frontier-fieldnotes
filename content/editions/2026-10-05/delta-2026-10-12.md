## Two research additions worth a visual

1. **OuroWorld — 8 October, v1.** Turns static Gaussian-splat scenes into looping 3D cinemagraphs using periodic deformation and a field that absorbs inconsistent generated views. Author evaluation covers 39 scenes with a limited ±20° orbit; seamless motion does not prove correct unseen geometry. Public code is available, but the full pipeline includes non-commercial dependencies. Its default reference-video path uses paid APIs; released references and a documented Wan 2.2 alternative are options. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12461v1), [repository](https://github.com/youzhe0305/OuroWorld). **Best next visual:** scrub a colored splat scene through one cycle, tune Fourier amplitude/phase and orbit range, then compare temporal closure with cross-view consistency. Proposed, not implemented.

2. **SepGen — 8 October, v1.** Joint generation produces video, a mixed soundtrack and individual captioned sound stems. It also separates a supplied mix. The reported evaluation is limited to two stems; separate tracks are not, by themselves, validated spatial audio. The public repository includes inference/training code, adapter links and a moving-camera demonstration; its stated setup requires a 48GB GPU and acceptance of the gated LTX-2.5 model terms. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11361v1), [repository](https://github.com/AviadDahan/SepGen). **Second visual:** an attention-routing diagram with video, mix and colored stem tokens; toggle masks and LoRA gates, then mute/rebalance synthetic stems. Show permitted information flow, not invented model-quality scores. Proposed, not implemented.

**Recommendation:** OuroWorld + periodic deformation. It adds a temporal mechanism to the existing Gaussian topic. A later implementation should expose both the loop seam and the limits of camera coverage, with the existing PNG/GIF sharing pattern. For a looping export, the camera must also stay fixed or return periodically; sample one cycle without duplicating the endpoint frame.
