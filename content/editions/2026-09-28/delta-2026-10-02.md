# October 2, 2026 — creative AI changes since September 28

Three new research candidates and two product-watch additions passed the primary-source check. Findings are author/vendor reports, not independent reproductions or a universal SOTA ranking. New visual ideas below are proposed, not implemented.

## Research worth a closer look

- **[WINGS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37816v1), submitted September 29, v1.** Completes missing Gaussian-splat regions using a 3D-native prior rather than reconciling separately inpainted reference views. The [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37816v1) evaluates 29 scenes; its approach has view-dependent appearance and alignment limitations. This is not real-time editing. A matching public runnable release was not verified. **Visual idea:** orbit a masked scene and compare inconsistent per-view fills with one shared 3D completion. Make the difference visible from a held-out camera.
- **[PartiCam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39504v1), submitted September 30, v1.** Uses global particle guidance and local refinement to steer pretrained video models toward a camera trajectory without retraining. The [paper's](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39504v1) small static/dynamic evaluation does not improve every camera metric. “Training-free” still incurs inference-time search cost. Runnable code was not verified; the author's listed project link resolves to an unrelated project. **Visual idea:** show candidate camera paths, reward weights, resampling ancestry and diversity collapse as the guidance budget changes.
- **[Audible World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38444v1), submitted September 29, v1.** Attaches generated sound to a reconstructed scene and renders audio along listener paths. The [method and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38444v1) describe an offline pipeline, static automatic geometry and fixed source placement; it does not establish dynamic action–sound synchronization. Evaluated on 80 generated scenes; code availability remains unverified. **Visual idea:** move a listener around sound emitters, inspect occlusion and left/right cues, and compare a fixed soundtrack with geometry-linked rendering.

**Recommended next build:** WINGS-inspired multi-view consistency. It expands the lab into the requested 3D Gaussian focus. PartiCam is a strong follow-up to the existing sampler lab; spatial audio offers a distinct filmmaking angle. Use analytic illustrations or synthetic scenes unless a paper implementation is actually run.

## Product changes

- **[Higgsfield Ads Studio](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/higgsfield-ads-studio), announced October 1.** Saved brand kits and product inputs drive batches of static-ad variations. The source describes current access, but account/plan availability and output quality were not tested. This is a workflow launch, not a new video model. A useful comparison would keep the product fixed and inspect how selling angle and brand constraints vary across a batch.
- **[OpenArt FLUX 3 Image](https://openart.ai/blog/flux-3-image-overview/), documented October 1.** The guide describes multi-reference generation/editing and 2K/4K output. [BFL's product page](https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-image) corroborates the model, but the guide date is not proof of its original global launch date or exact OpenArt integration time. Fidelity and edit-preservation claims still need testing. Compare reference roles, text preservation and identity consistency before turning this into a recommendation.

**Already covered:** [Eleven v4 and Turbo](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/eleven-v4) launched September 28. The announcement was updated October 1, but no identified change log establishes another model launch. Its new pronunciation/audio-tag guides are useful documentation, not a fresh capability announcement. ViRDM, FlashRender and VS-Splat remain at their previously tracked v1; no material post-baseline release was verified in the checked ViRDM/FlashRender repositories.
