## Kling 4.0 and an increasingly editable filmmaking workflow

[Higgsfield dates Kling 4.0 to October 21](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/changelog), with its [API catalog dated October 23](https://open.higgsfield.ai/models/kling-video/v4.0/text-to-video/playground). The dated specifications describe 3–30-second HD clips, up to ten keyframes and fifteen references. [OpenArt also lists it](https://openart.ai/ai-model/kling-4-0/), but its integration date is unknown. Undated or older pages advertise 4K; current dated Higgsfield specifications and OpenArt’s English instructions say 720p/1080p. Quality gains remain vendor claims.

Higgsfield’s October 22 **Content Factory** connects scripts, characters, scenes, voice lines and editing. A five-minute assembled project is not a five-minute native model generation. Plan limits and usable-shot cost were not tested. [Primary changelog](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/changelog)

**Visual angle:** keyframe constraints on a colored timeline, plus a dependency graph showing which shot or voice line must regenerate after an edit.

## Three new video/3D papers, all submitted after the last cutoff

- **[StreamEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28402), October 20:** evaluates evolving instructions, edits and preservation across 37 models. The best of three reported reference editors scores only 15/100 edit success. Low-scoring configurations were excluded from editing tables after evaluation; task scores are not a universal ranking. Code/data are available. **Visual:** requested, observed and preserved states on separate lanes.
- **[KoalaGS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28615), October 20:** allocates Gaussian detail by complexity and orders primitives for reduced budgets. Its transfer example is simulated on one scene; code is still coming soon. **Visual:** budget slider, complexity heatmap and error curve on a synthetic scene.
- **[SplatFlux](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28257), October 20:** decodes video latents into explicit static/dynamic Gaussians. Offline evaluations show tradeoffs between image quality and subject consistency. Code/weights are unverified. **Best new visual pairing:** freeze time, move the camera, then inspect static aggregation and moving splat interpolation. This would be an analytic teaching model, not a reproduction.

These are author-run evaluations, not independently reproduced SOTA results.

## Voice: availability, buffering and licenses matter

ElevenLabs’ [Realtime announcement](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/realtime) is October 20 and [Translation alpha](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/realtime-translation) October 21; the changelog groups both under October 19. These are newly discovered coverage, not October 23 launches. Realtime uses cascaded components and a WebSocket API; Translation is an enterprise alpha. [Independent benchmark evidence](https://artificialanalysis.ai/speech-to-speech) supports scoped Realtime results, not universal superiority. **Visual:** separate recognition, reasoning, synthesis and playback lanes; vary buffering and interruption timing.

[Phonon 0.4.0](https://github.com/gradium-ai/xn-ptts/releases/tag/v0.4.0) shipped October 20 at 13:36 UTC, after the previous review; model uploads date to October 19. It streams local speech. The 31M English model is Apache 2.0, while 40M variants are gated and CC BY-NC 4.0. Parameter counts exclude the audio tokenizer; quality comparisons are vendor-run.

[KittenTTS 2’s October 21 fix](https://github.com/KittenML/KittenTTS/commit/64baa6a675dd3e7725a44676796c36f58b95ede8) penalizes repeated silence tokens to address stalls and dropped narration. The model already existed; October 22 brought a demo, not its first release. Apache 2.0 code and [community-licensed weights](https://huggingface.co/KittenML/kitten-tts-2/blob/c93cb7c271baa51d9ae8204d5277988906f235a2/LICENSE.md) differ.

**Proposed visuals:** incremental audio buffers for Phonon; speech/silence/end-token sampling controls for KittenTTS. Neither was run locally.
