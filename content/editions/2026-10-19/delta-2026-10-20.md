## 1. Mise-en-Video: stage the shot in 3D

[October 19 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26801) combines projected 3D orientation maps with global/shot/subject attention. In the authors' 100-prompt evaluation, orientation error improves over MultiShotMaster and EchoShot; a larger HoloCine model still leads several quality measures. Separate generation runs can change identity. Code and weights were not verified; this is not an announced Kling feature.

**Proposed visual:** rotate colored actor boxes, switch cameras, and inspect the projected conditioning maps and attention scopes. The same 2D rectangle can hide different facing directions. Illustrative projection, not generated footage.

## 2. Wan-Alpha++: make the effect appear and disappear

[October 19 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26189) extends earlier Wan-Alpha to reference-conditioned RGBA effects. A phase shift brings visible content to the conditioning boundary before restoring the transparent-visible-transparent timeline. Authors evaluate 60 image-conditioned videos with 40 sampling steps; visible-frame appearance scores and selected two-stage baselines do not establish universal VFX superiority. The [official repository](https://github.com/WeChatCV/Wan-Alpha) still marks I2V weights pending. Earlier four-step T2V results do not describe this path.

**Proposed visual:** move a reference frame along an alpha timeline, adjust phase and opacity, and composite over different backgrounds. An empty first frame offers little appearance information.

## 3. FluidGen: appearance and motion need different checks

[October 19 UTC paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25660) jointly predicts RGB and physical velocity, then uses motion-based preference training for smoke/fire VFX. Tests cover 15 FluidNexus sequences and 20 selected images. Preference training improves an imaging-quality score while several fidelity measures worsen. This is a learned plausibility signal, not enforcement of Navier–Stokes. [Code/data are coming soon](https://y-zheng18.github.io/FluidGen/).

**Best next deep visual:** advect colored smoke through a numerical 2D field with curl, divergence, viscosity and time-step controls. Place a separate latent sampling path beside it. Physical velocity and flow-matching velocity live in different spaces. Proposed teaching simulation, not FluidGen inference.

## 4. Ming TTS: serving does not guarantee incremental speech

The [initial inference-code commit](https://github.com/inclusionAI/Ming-3.0-tts/commit/14c6e082161b7d8263909cfba2829661cc5cedc2) is October 19; both checkpoint uploads date to September 24. Tiny's [serving instructions](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ming-3.0-tts-tiny/commit/b170071302cb0b06435ea784787b119f40a08be8) were added October 20 at 03:27 UTC, after our review. Voice design, reference synthesis and expression controls use separate modes. Author benchmark results vary by language and task; the live independent leaderboard could not be verified.

The [SGLang-Omni cookbook](https://sgl-project.github.io/sglang-omni/cookbook/ming3_tts.html) explicitly returns a finished waveform even with `stream=true`. This interface does not establish parity with all reference-code controls. No inference or listening test was run.

**Proposed visual:** separate latent generation, decoding, buffering and playback lanes; vary chunking and release policy to show time to first playable audio.
