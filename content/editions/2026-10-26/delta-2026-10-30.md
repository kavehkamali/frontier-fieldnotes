2. **L3G-World: persistent Gaussian memory for camera-directed video.** Submitted **October 29, 17:54:42 UTC**. Gaussians store image and latent features; direct latent rendering avoids re-encoding RGB conditioning. Author tests use 481-frame out-and-back paths on DL3DV/RE10K. Revisiting a consistent generated scene does not prove its geometry is true. Dynamic objects remain a limitation, memory grows with explored area, and code/checkpoints are pending. The reported 359× gain concerns conditioning, not total video generation. **Best proposed graphic:** orbit out and back, toggle Gaussian spawning/refinement, and plot explored area against memory and separate conditioning cost. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40614v1) · [Release status](https://github.com/cvlab-kaist/L3G-World).

Seven seconds measures transformer computation, not end-to-end delivery; quality comparisons are vendor-run. Model adapters retain the MiniMax community license. **Visual angle:** distilled teacher/student trajectories plus a timing breakdown. This does not change our Turbo 8 generation preference. 4. **SonoWorld4D: separate the source sound from the moving listener.** Submitted **October 29, 17:58:45 UTC**. It combines dynamic Gaussians with synchronized object/camera audio diffusion. The author's 54-scene benchmark uses additional panoramas and tracked object views; a 50-person study covers nine scenes. Direct-path audio excludes reflections/reverberation, and real-time rendering refers to prepared scenes. Code is coming soon. **Visual angle:** drag source/listener paths and inspect emission time, arrival time, Doppler and attenuation; explain why reusing a shifted recording can double-apply motion. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40640v1) · [Code status](https://github.com/HuMathe/sonoworld4d).
