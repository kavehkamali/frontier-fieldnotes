## 1. Frontier watchlist — in priority order

1. **[LLaCO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32604v1) · 23 October, v1.** New to our coverage, submitted before Friday’s cutoff. It combines spherical corruption with a schedule based on token competition. Authors evaluate nine benchmarks against selected 7–8B diffusion-language models: MATH is 62.12 versus Dream’s 51.20, while HumanEval is 48.17 versus 54.27. That is a tradeoff, not universal superiority. Code and weights are still TODOs in the [official repository](https://github.com/WhenGIX/LLaCO). **Creator angle:** a precise way to explain text diffusion for scripts and structured writing; no evidence of filmmaking gains. **Best teaching pairing this week.**

2. **[FastH3 Omni](https://haoailab.com/blogs/fastvideo-omniref-update/) · 20 October announcement.** Thirty-two internal predictions are grouped into eight transformer calls for reference-conditioned video/audio. The author’s complete-serving benchmark covers 27 workloads on eight GB200s and reports 5.6–11.9× lower end-to-end latency than the 50-evaluation base. Serving recipes differ and quality parity is not established; do not transfer the result to a consumer GPU. [Weights and documentation](https://huggingface.co/FastVideo/FastVideo-FastH3-Omni-8-Step-V1) are public under the MiniMax-H3 community license. Files predate the announcement. **Creator angle:** faster reference-guided iteration is promising, but call count, final-shot quality and total latency are separate. No change to the user’s Turbo 8 generation preference.

3. **[Direct Forcing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30861v1) · 22 October, v1.** A single post-training stage teaches both paired continuation and the distribution of generated rollouts. Authors start from Wan2.1-1.3B and use 250 updates on eight B200s. On 944 VBench prompts with five samples each, total score is 84.63 versus Elastic Forcing’s 84.25, but semantic score is lower, 79.08 versus 80.99. Four evaluations apply per causal chunk; the update count excludes backbone training. No official runnable release was verified. **Creator angle:** useful research toward responsive video, not a new production tool.

4. **[UniVOE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31641v1) · 22 October paper; 21 October weight uploads.** An effect-aware remover creates paired training data, then injection learns the reverse edit—including shadows and reflections. Author tests use 100 VOR and 56 ROSE pairs; ROSE injection FVD is 85.12 versus Seedance 2.0’s 143.56 in the selected comparison. The reward model is proprietary, and filled backgrounds remain plausible guesses. [Public weight files](https://huggingface.co/Ysz2022/UniVOE) exist, but the linked code repo returns 404 and the model card’s license section is a placeholder despite Apache metadata. **Creator angle:** cleanup and product insertion need interaction-aware editing, with access and licensing still incomplete.

5. **[SplatFlux](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28257v1) · 20 October, v1; retained from Friday.** A shared decoder turns observed or generated video latents into static/dynamic 4D Gaussians. Reconstruction and generation are tested across several datasets. VBench-I2V imaging score is 72.07 versus Aether’s 69.85, while subject consistency is lower, 95.95 versus 97.97. This is offline research using a Wan2.1-14B adaptation; proxy video scores do not prove universal 4D superiority. No runnable code or weights verified. **Creator angle:** re-framing a moving scene after generation is the interesting direction. No new revision is claimed this week.

6. **[Ideogram Vector on Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/changelog) · 23 October host integration.** [Official API documentation](https://developer.ideogram.ai/api-reference/vectors/generate-svg) separates prompt-to-SVG generation from image vectorization. That is a useful output change, not a measured quality win. SVG paths do not automatically mean semantic layers, live text or clean topology. Hosted access is documented; no output was generated or inspected. The underlying model’s exact launch date was not established from a dated primary announcement. **Creator angle:** inspect grouping and lettering before using generated assets in title sequences or motion graphics.

7. **[Phonon v0.4.1–v0.4.2](https://github.com/gradium-ai/xn-ptts/releases/tag/v0.4.2) · 24–25 October.** These are verified changes after Friday’s review. [v0.4.1](https://github.com/gradium-ai/xn-ptts/releases/tag/v0.4.1) exposes normalized spoken text before loading a model. v0.4.2 adds narrower-decoder support, Mac Catalyst and speech regression checks. The attached model bundles are unchanged; regression transcription is not a listening/prosody benchmark. Existing 31M English weights are Apache 2.0; 40M variants remain gated and noncommercial. **Creator angle:** check how dates, prices and abbreviations will be spoken before rendering narration.

8. **[Runway App Studio](https://runway.com/changelog) · 23 October.** A hosted workflow release: describe a creative app, choose models per step and download published source. Official documentation says paid plans and credit-consuming build sessions; we did not build an app or measure productivity. The [current guide](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/56447349336467-Building-apps-with-App-Studio) also says apps cannot be deleted or unpublished through App Studio; audience restriction is the documented alternative. **Creator angle:** evaluate selective reruns, portability and cost for repeated storyboard or review tasks. This is not a new generative-model benchmark.

## 2. Fundamentals to turn into interactive graphics

1. **A smooth path can hide a sudden identity change — Interactive ready.** **Graphic:** colored token vectors, a two-dimensional projection and a rank plot computed in the original dimension. **Controls:** dimension (8/32/128/512), vocabulary, seed, noising progress, linear/spherical path, step budget and shrink-without-noise preset; PNG/GIF export. Equal/adaptive grids and rank comparisons are shown together. **Misconception:** equal geometric steps remove equal semantic information. **Recent connection:** [LLaCO, 23 October](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32604v1). Cosine geometry and spherical interpolation are older foundations. **Audience/recommendation:** ML engineers and curious creators; lead this week.

2. **Eight calls can contain thirty-two updates — Proposed.** **Graphic:** a fine trajectory grid grouped into fused calls, beside encoding/sampling/decoding time lanes. **Controls:** grid resolution, predictions per call, call cost, fixed overhead and approximation error. **Misconception:** every solver update is a full network call, or a call-count reduction guarantees the same hardware speedup. **Recent connection:** [FastH3 Omni, 20 October](https://haoailab.com/blogs/fastvideo-omniref-update/). **Audience/recommendation:** video creators and ML engineers; strongest next mechanism lesson. Analytic costs would be assumptions, not benchmark results.

3. **Following a sequence and matching a population are different jobs — Proposed.** **Graphic:** reference/generated feature clouds plus paired continuation arrows. **Controls:** kernel bandwidth, loss weights, history perturbation, diversity and toy optimization steps. **Misconception:** matching a video population guarantees the right next event for one history. **Recent connection:** [Direct Forcing, 22 October](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30861v1), applying older MMD foundations. **Audience/recommendation:** video-model engineers; explain complementary training signals without simulating a trained video model.

4. **The shadow belongs to the edit too — Proposed.** **Graphic:** connected object, shadow, reflection and background layers. **Controls:** light direction, position, masks, dilation and object-only/effect-aware removal. **Misconception:** an object edit only changes its visible pixels, or inpainting reveals the true hidden background. **Recent connection:** [UniVOE, 22 October](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31641v1). **Audience/recommendation:** compositors and filmmakers; the most immediately legible practical lesson. Use synthetic compositing, not purported model output.

5. **Freeze time. Move the camera — Proposed; retained from Friday.** **Graphic:** static and moving Gaussian primitives with independent time and camera controls. **Controls:** orbit, time, static/dynamic threshold, velocity arrows, interpolation and occlusion. **Misconception:** a plausible generated video already contains a reusable 4D scene. **Recent connection:** [SplatFlux, 20 October](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28257v1). **Audience/recommendation:** 3D artists and graphics engineers; next 3D lesson after the existing streaming lab. No learned reconstruction would be claimed.

6. **Sharp edges do not make an editable scene — Proposed.** **Graphic:** one original graphic shown as pixels, polygons and Bézier paths, with grouping and live-text layers exposed. **Controls:** zoom, curve tolerance, node budget, selection mode and outlined text. **Misconception:** SVG automatically provides meaningful layers, editable typography or small files. **Recent connection:** [Ideogram Vector integration, 23 October](https://ideogram.ai/models/vector/). **Audience/recommendation:** motion designers; a compact lesson in older vector/raster foundations prompted by a new feature.

7. **The voice reads a different script — Proposed.** **Graphic:** typed-script and normalized-script lanes before audio latents, decoding and playback. **Controls:** language, date/price format, abbreviations, text diff, frame duration and buffer. **Misconception:** a better voice fixes the wrong interpretation of written text, or a runtime patch means new voices. **Recent connection:** [Phonon normalization API, 24 October](https://github.com/gradium-ai/xn-ptts/releases/tag/v0.4.1), followed by v0.4.2 on 25 October. **Audience/recommendation:** narration creators; practical preflight lesson. Example transformations need labeling unless tested against the actual API.

8. **Change one line. Which assets must change? — Proposed.** **Graphic:** script, references, shots, voice and final-edit dependency graph with downstream invalidation. **Controls:** dependency links, changed input, caching, retries and assumed per-node cost. **Misconception:** every edit requires a full rerender, or assembled film duration equals one model’s native generation length. **Recent connection:** [Runway App Studio, 23 October](https://runway.com/changelog) and [Higgsfield Content Factory, 22 October](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/changelog). **Audience/recommendation:** AI filmmakers and tool builders; compare repeatable workflows, not unmeasured productivity claims.

## Evidence and presentation limits

Public Reddit supplied discovery leads. Author announcements, unstructured user timings and preference comments are labeled as such; they do not establish independent quality or speed results. [Source coverage](source-coverage.md) records all checked communities, gaps and the exact release-date distinctions. The public-only Reddit tool failed its listing access, so discovery used partial search indexing rather than a complete feed.

The implemented lab uses seeded synthetic vectors, a display projection and explicit geometric calculations. Rank is computed in the full vector space. Its semantic schedule is a toy construction, not LLaCO’s calibrated 8B schedule. It does not generate or denoise text and is not a reproduction. Every other visual is a proposal. The light, minimal presentation uses color to distinguish mechanisms, not to imply measured performance. Publishing drafts are prepared for review; nothing is posted to social accounts.
