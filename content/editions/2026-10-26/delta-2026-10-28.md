1. **ReferStream — 27 October, 17:12:42 UTC.** Adds a reference subject during streaming video by temporarily injecting reference features into global KV slots, generating the arrival chunk, then restoring the protected entries while keeping new history. The benchmark contains 68 curated entries; comparators receive different reference information and the collection was used during development. No independent reproduction. The public repository contains a website and recorded demos, not inference code. **Visual angle:** scrub a colored cache timeline through snapshot, injection, generation and restoration; distinguish restored memory from rolling back emitted video. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37566v1) · [Full evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37566v1) · [Repository](https://github.com/Xushuolin/ReferStream).

2. **Honeycomb-Surfels / Hexels — 27 October, 17:59:59 UTC.** Hexagonal surface patches carry a local color gradient. Authors compare 34 scenes; their memory-matched quality comparisons and primitive-matched speed comparisons use different conditions. Matched model bytes do not mean equal training VRAM. Real trainer/kernel/viewer code is present; a linked checkpoint collection was confirmed by Hub metadata, but its file tree and weights were not inspected. Inherited dependency licenses need separate attention. **Best proposed visual:** independently toggle footprint shape and constant/linear color on a synthetic surface, then vary texture, curvature and camera angle. That makes the mechanism and its limits visible without pretending to reproduce the paper. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37668v1) · [Methods and evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37668v1) · [Code](https://github.com/honeycomb-surfels/hexels) · [Model collection](https://huggingface.co/jhu-ccvl/Hexels).
